Like is the project about the aesthetic, the space, or the aesthetic that develops because of the space



To me its about the appropriation and deconstruction and ambiguity of symbols, Im really interested in that both artistically and philosophically and I think graffiti is like the best medium to explore that through because its already like a closed set of symbols that are only known relative to a small culture and even in that is understood differently from each person to the next. Also its symbology is entirely vague to people not involved. So whats really cool to me about the book is that were feeding graffiti through so many layers of subjectivity and therefore deconstruction, from graffiti removed a degree to pictures of it, then to writing about it that is not entirely about it, and then to the audience.
